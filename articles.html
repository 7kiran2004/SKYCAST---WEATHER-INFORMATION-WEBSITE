☁️ How Clouds Form

Warm, moist air rises and cools. Water vapour condenses on dust particles, forming droplets that gather into clouds.

🌈 Why Rainbows Appear

Sunlight refracts and reflects inside raindrops, splitting into colours from red to violet.
